NPYDF News & Updates – Stories from Kasenengwa

Stay updated with activities, success stories, announcements and events from National Pride for Youth Development Foundation. Field stories coordinated by Project Manager Jessy Nyirongo and Fundraising & Communications Manager Marcel Banda.

Announcement · 02 Nov 2026

Organizational restructuring complete

NPYDF has completed an organizational restructuring to strengthen management, coordination and donor-readiness. Chairperson Francis Banda led the process, with Jessy Nyirongo coordinating programs and Marcel Banda supporting fundraising and communications.

Category: Announcement · Author: Marcel Banda, Fundraising & Communications Manager

Pride Farms · 28 Oct 2026

Harvest success in Kasenengwa

Youth farmers report a demonstration-plot harvest of maize and practical climate-smart agriculture training. The team is planning next steps for a poultry pass-on initiative.

Category: Pride Farms · Author: Ishmael Banda, Operations Manager

Pride Lady · 20 Oct 2026

20 women complete SILC training

Twenty women completed Village Savings and Loans training and reported collective first-month savings. The program supports economic empowerment and awareness of gender-based violence prevention.

Category: Pride Lady · Author: Yvonne Mumba, HR Manager

Innovation Hub · 15 Oct 2026

Innovation Hub receives five computers

Five refurbished computers were received to support digital literacy training for learners. Internet connectivity support is still needed to expand access and learning opportunities.

Category: Innovation Hub · Author: Jessy Nyirongo, Project Manager

12 youth graduate in tailoring and carpentry
