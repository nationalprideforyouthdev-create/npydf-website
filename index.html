Who we are

Young people shaping a stronger Chipata

NPYDF Chipata is a youth-development initiative working with communities, schools and partners to help young people access opportunity and make a positive contribution.

Our vision

A proud, skilled and self-reliant generation of young people.

Our mission

To develop young people through skills training, agriculture, innovation, mentorship and community action.

Our motto: “Building a pride of empowered youths. one youth at a time”

Our commitment

Opportunity. Skills. Service. Pride.

We believe every young person deserves the chance to learn, contribute and build a sustainable future.

Project detail · Girls' empowerment

Pride Lady

Supporting girls through mentorship, health information, menstrual health support and school engagement.

Areas of support

  • Mentorship and confidence building
  • Health and wellbeing awareness
  • Menstrual health education and pads support
  • School support and referrals

Schools & leadership

Project lead: Yvonne Mumba

Community links include Chipata Day, Hillside Girls, Anoya Zulu, Kapata and Mchini schools. Activities should be coordinated with school authorities and safeguarding procedures.
