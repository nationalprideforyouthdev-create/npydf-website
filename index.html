Who we are

About NPYDF

Building a generation of skilled, confident, responsible and empowered young people.

Our vision

Young people thriving. Communities flourishing.

Our vision is a society where young people are empowered, productive, included and actively contributing to sustainable national development.

Our mission

Empowerment through action.

Our mission is to support youth development through education, skills, leadership, innovation, entrepreneurship, partnerships and community-led initiatives.

Our values

Our strategic focus

Turning our mission into practical action

Our work connects personal development with practical opportunities and community participation.

Build capabilities

Strengthen foundational, digital, vocational, leadership and enterprise skills that help young people make informed choices.

Expand opportunity

Connect participants to learning, mentorship, networks, information and pathways to employment or self-employment as opportunities become available.

Strengthen communities

Encourage youth-led service, inclusive participation, local problem-solving and collaboration with community stakeholders.

Accountability

Responsible and respectful development

NPYDF is committed to treating participants with dignity, encouraging safe and inclusive participation, handling enquiries responsibly and sharing verified information about activities and results. Organizational policies, leadership profiles and approved reports can be added here as they are formally confirmed.

Our approach

  • Listen to young people and communities.
  • Design practical, locally relevant activities.
  • Work with partners to expand opportunity.
  • Learn, measure progress and improve.
  • Promote safe, inclusive and accountable participation.
