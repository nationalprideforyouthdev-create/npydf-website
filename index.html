About NPYDF

National Pride for Youth Development Foundation — Chipata, Zambia.

Who We Are

We are a youth-focused community development foundation supporting young people through practical skills, mentorship, farming, innovation and community action.

Our Vision

A proud, skilled, self-reliant generation.

Our Mission

To empower young people with practical skills, opportunities, leadership and community-led solutions that strengthen livelihoods and local development.

Our Motto

“Building a pride of empowered youths. One youth at a time.”

Our Communities

Chipata Central, Kapata, Mchini, Nabvutika, Chimwemwe, Chinjala, Kasenengwa, Madzimoyo, Msanga and Walela.
