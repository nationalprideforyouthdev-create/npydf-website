Who we are

About NPYDF Chipata

National Pride for Youth Development Foundation is a youth-focused organisation working to help young people become skilled, confident and self-reliant contributors to society.

🌟

Our Vision

A proud, skilled and self-reliant generation of young people who actively contribute to the development of their communities and Zambia.

🎯

Our Mission

To develop young people through practical skills, agriculture, innovation, mentorship, entrepreneurship and community participation.

National pride

Promoting responsible citizenship, service and love for our communities.

Empowerment

Creating opportunities for youth to learn, grow and become self-reliant.

Integrity & teamwork

Working with honesty, respect, inclusion and shared responsibility.

Where we work

NPYDF activities are focused in Chipata District and surrounding communities, including Chipata Central, Kapata, Mchini, Nabvutika, Chimwemwe, Chinjala, Kasenengwa, Madzimoyo, Msanga and Walela.

Our work

Five projects. One shared purpose.

Our projects respond to practical needs and help young people build skills, confidence and opportunities.

🧵
Skills training

Pride Pathways

Tailoring, carpentry, ICT and entrepreneurship training to improve employability and support self-employment.

Project lead: Artness Banda
🌸
Girls empowerment

Pride Lady

Mentorship, health awareness, menstrual health support, reusable pads and school support for girls.

Community links include Chipata Day, Hillside Girls, Anoya Zulu, Kapata and Mchini schools.

Project lead: Yvonne Mumba
🌾
Agriculture

Pride Farms

Maize, soya, vegetables and poultry activities supporting food production and youth agribusiness.

Farming areas: Kasenengwa, Madzimoyo and Msanga. Markets: Chipata Main Market and Kapata Market.

Project lead: Victor Mwale
💡
Digital skills

Innovation Hub

Computer skills, phone repair, social media for business, coding and digital content creation in Chipata Town.

Project lead: Marcell Banda
🌳
Community action

Pride Works

Community clean-ups and tree planting, including activities around Chipata Bus Station, Kapata Market and the Hospital Area.

Project lead: Memory Lungu
🚀

Our shared goal

Help young people turn potential into practical skills, sustainable livelihoods and visible community contribution.
